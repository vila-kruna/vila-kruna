How to get here?

From the Ibar Highway, take the Rakovica exit on the ring road, drive straight and on your right side after approximately one kilometer you will see a signpost for Vila Kruna. Vila Kruna is located in a quiet side street with plenty of free parking space in the immediate vicinity.
